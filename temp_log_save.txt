The website does **not** change any letters to underscores. Because of this, the files inside your Google Drive are actually named with underscores (for example, `A hming fakin zai r_u.pdf`). When the website imports the folder from Google Drive, it simply imports the exact filename that Google Drive gives it.

1. Open your Google Drive folder where you keep the PDFs.
2. Find the files with underscores (like `A hming fakin zai r_u.pdf` or files where `ṭ` was replaced).
3. Right-click them, select **Rename**, and type the `'` and `ṭ` back in manually inside Google Drive.
4. Go to your website's Admin Dashboard, delete the old songs, and click **Import Folder** again.
